Our Heritage

A Century of Quiet Grandeur

Founded in 1928 beside Lahore's green belt, FAIRWAY ROYALE remains a members-only club where championship golf, hospitality and discretion are held to the same standard.

Today the club stands in DHA Phase 9, Lahore, Pakistan — still private, still exacting, and still devoted to the game as it should be played.

Members Lounge

The Quiet Hours

A private room above the eighteenth in DHA Phase 9, Lahore, for members who prefer their conversation as considered as their golf.

Firelight, a small library, and a terrace that looks back across the finishing hole. Evening menus are written daily; jackets are preferred after dusk.

Access is reserved for Gold and Royale members, and for accompanied Green members on Friday evenings.
